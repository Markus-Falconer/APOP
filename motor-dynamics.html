Assumptions and limits

  • Sleeve: thin ring over a rigid shaft, with a magnet that has no hoop strength (assume it is cracked). Good to first order for t/r up to about 0.15. A thicker sleeve, a soft core, or a final design needs an orthotropic shrink-fit calculation or FEA, including axial retention and end caps.
  • Field: the 2-pole model is a uniformly magnetised (diametric) cylinder inside a smooth iron bore with a non-magnetic shaft. The 4-pole and higher model is a surface-magnet circuit with an 85% pole arc. Kv is scaled from the donor's rated Kv by the field ratio, so absolute field errors largely cancel. Saturation and armature reaction are ignored.
  • Kv convention: rpm per volt of line-line peak back-EMF, which is about the no-load DC bus voltage of an ESC. Kt = 60/(2π·Kv) in N·m per DC-link amp.
  • Losses do not include eddy currents in the magnet or sleeve. These are driven by slot ripple and rectifier current harmonics, and they matter because a composite sleeve insulates the magnet thermally. Segment the magnet axially and avoid metal sleeves at these speeds. Iron loss uses catalogue loss densities: replace them with the datasheet curve for the real lamination.
  • Rotordynamics: rotor as a lumped mass on a two-bearing shaft, with no gyroscopic effects, coupling or overhung mass. It screens for gross problems. Confirm with a rotordynamics code and a spin test.
  • Starting: spool drag torque and the speed the starter must reach are placeholders. Measure them from an ECU start log (starter current and rpm against time).
  • Default dimensions for the TP 4040-SCM are estimates from the catalogue size (40 × 74 mm, 2-pole). Measure the stator bore, magnet, sleeve, shaft, bearings, resistance and inductance of the real motor.
