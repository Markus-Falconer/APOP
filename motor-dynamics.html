Assumptions and limits

  • Lehner data (A. Lehner, 23 Nov 2026): magnet 14.7 mm OD × 5 mm bore, glued segments; glued carbon sleeve, 14.9 mm OD (0.1 mm wall); steel EZO bearings, about 5 × 10 mm; balance G1 at a minimum of 100,000 rpm. Kv = 1441 rpm/V in delta for the /17 (about 24,500 ÷ wind, from Lehner's and MGM's LMT 1535 tables). Generate in delta: star gives about 192 V at 160k, too close to the ICD's 200 V.
  • Estimates to replace: stator bore 15.5 mm (0.3 mm radial clearance), 35 mm stack, 26 mm lamination OD, 50 mm bearing span, line-line resistance and inductance scaled by Kv² from a reference winding, and the 20 A continuous limit. The 1525 assumes a 25 mm stack and 40 mm span, with a wind of about /24 so its Kv matches the /17 (Kv ∝ 1/(turns · length)); its resistance scales as turns² × (stack + 10 mm end turns).
  • Sleeve (3.1)–(3.6): thin ring over a rigid shaft, with a magnet that has no hoop strength. That matches glued segments. Lehner's sleeve is glued, so "contact pressure kept at speed" defaults to 0 and the burst estimate (3.6) uses no preload and no safety factor. A pressed-on sleeve with interference keeps the segments clamped. Good to first order for t/r up to about 0.15; a final design needs an orthotropic shrink-fit calculation or FEA.
  • Field (4.1)–(4.3): uniformly magnetised (diametric) cylinder inside a smooth iron bore. Kv is scaled from the rated Kv by the field ratio, so absolute field errors largely cancel. Saturation and armature reaction are ignored.
  • Kv convention (5.2): rpm per volt of line-line peak back-EMF, about the no-load DC voltage of a bridge. Kt = 60/(2π·Kv) in N·m per DC-link amp.
  • Losses (7.1)–(7.4) leave out eddy currents in the magnet and sleeve, driven by slot ripple and rectifier current harmonics. The Pfister and Perriard prototype lost about 2× its modelled losses: budget for that.
  • Rotordynamics (9.1)–(9.4): rotor as a lumped mass on a two-bearing shaft, with no gyroscopic effects and no coupler. The motor-side coupler half adds an overhung mode not shown here (about 240k rpm for 10 g at 10 mm overhang, about 130k for 20 g at 15 mm). Lehner reports that rotors fail from coupling vibration. Confirm with a tap test and a spin test.
  • Bearings (8.1): EZO's stainless catalogue rates 5 mm bore bearings at 50,000–54,000 rpm (5×11 and 5×13); a 5×10 is likely about 55–60k. The rule-of-thumb DN ceilings here are coarse; check the bearing maker's rating.
  • Starting (11.1)–(11.3): defaults follow the ESC shortlist: 12S (50.4 V) starter ESC and a 15 A current limit (half the coupler's measured pull-out torque). Spool drag and handoff speed are placeholders: measure them from an ECU start log. The stock starter is the RK-370SD-5028 (Kv from published no-load speeds; 0.25 Ω estimated).
  • TP 4040 presets keep the earlier catalogue-size estimates (40 × 74 mm, 2-pole).
